8. TRAINER FIT ENGINE
==================================================

There must be ONE authoritative trainer-fit service.

Hard eligibility must be evaluated BEFORE scoring.

Check:

- trainer role
- active status
- approval status
- competency coverage
- mandatory competencies
- availability
- conflicts
- capacity

Default configurable scoring:

Competency Coverage = 40%
Domain Alignment = 25%
Experience = 15%
Qualification = 10%
Observed Training Effectiveness = 10%

Formula:

score =
coverage * 0.40
+ domain_alignment * 0.25
+ experience * 0.15
+ qualification * 0.10
+ effectiveness * 0.10

IMPORTANT:

These weights are implementation defaults, not official SIH weights.

Store the scoring policy/version centrally.

Do not have multiple conflicting scoring formulas in different files.

Every match must explain:

- total score
- competency coverage
- domain alignment
- experience
- qualification
- effectiveness
- eligibility results
- covered competencies
- missing competencies
- availability
- capacity

Call it:

TRAINER FIT SCORE

Do not call it AI confidence.
